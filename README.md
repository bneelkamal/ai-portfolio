# AI Portfolio

A curated portfolio of practical AI products, agentic systems, and research-oriented applications by **Neelkamal Badana**.

## Profile

I am a **Product Manager with 14 years of experience** and a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence, aspiring to pursue doctoral research at the intersection of AI and business management.

My work combines product strategy, stakeholder communication, business-value analysis, AI system design, and responsible deployment. This portfolio demonstrates how emerging AI capabilities can be translated into practical products for business decision-making.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

- Identifying valuable business problems and translating them into AI opportunities.
- Designing AI products around users, workflows, risks, and measurable outcomes.
- Connecting research ideas with practical prototypes and deployable demonstrations.
- Communicating AI architectures and tradeoffs to both technical and executive audiences.
- Evaluating systems for accuracy, usefulness, explainability, reliability, and responsible adoption.

## Project standard

Each project will include:

1. Business problem and target users.
2. Product opportunity and expected value.
3. Stakeholder and workflow considerations.
4. AI system architecture and data flow.
5. MVP scope and explicit non-goals.
6. Technical and product evaluation.
7. Working demo or reproducible walkthrough.
8. Limitations, ethical considerations, and future work.

## Roadmap

Projects are prioritized by employer value, research relevance, demo clarity, and implementation effort. The initial portfolio will favor focused prototypes with small or synthetic datasets, deterministic baselines, transparent evaluations, and deployable Streamlit demonstrations before adding more advanced agentic capabilities.

The planned sequence is:

1. AI Data Analyst.
2. RAG Document Intelligence Assistant.
3. Explainable Fraud Detection.
4. Agentic Business Research Assistant.
5. Responsible AI Evaluation Toolkit.
