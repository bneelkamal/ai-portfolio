# Portfolio Projects

This directory contains project-level documentation and shared portfolio material. Larger applications may later live in dedicated repositories and be linked from the main portfolio README.

## Standard project format

Each project should explain:

1. **Business problem** — Who has the problem and why it matters.
2. **Product opportunity** — What decision, workflow, or user experience can be improved.
3. **Users and stakeholders** — Primary users, business owners, technical teams, and risk stakeholders.
4. **AI architecture** — Models, agents, tools, data flow, and human-oversight boundaries.
5. **MVP scope** — What is intentionally included and excluded.
6. **Evaluation plan** — Technical metrics plus usefulness, trust, latency, and risk.
7. **Working demo** — Streamlit app, screenshots, short video, or reproducible notebook.
8. **Product reflection** — Tradeoffs, adoption barriers, governance concerns, and next steps.

## Planned projects

### 1. AI Data Analyst

A Streamlit application that accepts Excel/CSV files, infers column types, performs automated EDA, recommends suitable charts, and produces executive-friendly summaries. The initial version will use deterministic analysis and a lightweight multi-agent orchestration layer.

### 2. RAG Document Intelligence Assistant

A small enterprise-style document question-answering application demonstrating document ingestion, chunking, retrieval, source citations, grounded responses, and evaluation.

### 3. Explainable Fraud Detection

A compact risk-analytics workflow demonstrating class-imbalance handling, anomaly detection, threshold selection, model evaluation, and interpretable risk explanations.

### 4. Agentic Business Research Assistant

A multi-agent workflow that plans a research task, gathers evidence, synthesizes findings, and performs a review step against unsupported claims.

### 5. Responsible AI Evaluation Toolkit

A practical toolkit for checking AI systems for reliability, traceability, explainability, selected fairness risks, and governance readiness.

## Prioritization principle

Projects are prioritized by business value, research relevance, demo clarity, and implementation effort. The objective is not to build unnecessarily large products; it is to present focused systems with clear product reasoning, credible evidence, and honest limitations.
